A few things to know

Your records are entered by you.

KoboLedger does not automatically connect to your bank or verify a cash balance. It shows the information you enter, so review amounts and dates and compare them with your receipts or account statements when accuracy matters. Keep your sign-in details private. Shared household access is available where the workspace supports it; share records only with people you trust.

KoboLedger is a record-keeping tool, not a bank, lender, investment service or substitute for professional financial advice. Check the app for current features and plan availability.
