School context

One product still requires different implementation decisions.

School type alone does not determine fit. Curriculum, age group, teacher workload, review standards, devices, connectivity, privacy responsibilities, and staff readiness should shape the workflow.

Primary and Montessori

Check age-appropriate language, practical activity, observation, short lesson flow, and available classroom materials.

Secondary and Cambridge

Check subject depth, syllabus terminology, practical work, assessment alignment, and the school's required lesson format.

Private and Government

Check approval routes, staff capacity, device access, training, privacy, support ownership, and the cost of sustained adoption.

Evaluate fit

Review the complete product scope and begin with a limited, teacher-reviewed pilot tied to a measurable school need.
