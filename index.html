A clear path to everyday use

From first session to work your team can apply.

Training is designed to connect what people learn with the work they return to.

  1. 01Assess

    Understand current confidence, roles and priority tasks.

  2. 02Train

    Practise with guided demonstrations and accessible tools.

  3. 03Apply

    Complete real workplace projects with facilitator support.

  4. 04Evaluate

    Review the work and assess understanding before certification.

  5. 05Keep working

    Continue with JENECONK tools and a 30-day practice challenge.

Management can receive a summary of assessment scores, completed projects and opportunities to improve everyday workflows.

Practical AI and automation training for church administrators.

RCCG Rivers Family Administrators from Regions 33, 5 and 55 completed a two-day programme on AI-assisted research, reports, meeting minutes, presentations and administrative workflows.

Twenty-two participants submitted evaluations. All respondents said they would recommend the programme. The published story explains the training, what was practised and the limits of the evidence.
