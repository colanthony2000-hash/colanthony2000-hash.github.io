Local planning context

Connect the software decision to real education responsibilities.

The FCT Education Secretariat is responsible for domesticating national education policy to FCT needs and includes bodies for universal basic education, secondary education, resources, planning and quality assurance. A school technology plan should support those formal responsibilities without implying government approval of a private product.

JENECONK offers distinct pathways rather than one oversized school-management claim. Edu Suite supports reviewed academic documents; AI Study Coach supports independent examination practice; JEMS supports English mastery; the Academy develops practical skills; and CBT is a scoped institutional service.

Pilot design

Prove usefulness with one controlled workflow.

Choose one workflow whose present cost is known, such as lesson-note review or term-report preparation. Agree the expected format and approval owner, train a limited group, and compare quality and turnaround time against the previous process.

  1. Document the current process and its known problems.
  2. Name the staff who will use, review and support the workflow.
  3. Agree what information may and may not enter the system.
  4. Train with the school's own approved format and realistic examples.
  5. Review evidence before renewing, expanding or connecting another product.

Delivery position

Know what location support actually means.

JENECONK can provide remote product guidance and discuss scheduled delivery in Abuja. Onsite availability is subject to a written engagement scope; no FCT office or government affiliation is implied.

Do not send passwords, learner records, examination questions, payment details or confidential staff information through the general contact form. Begin with a non-sensitive brief covering school type, curriculum, number of intended users, current workflow, delivery preference and timing.

Implementation checklist

Prepare a brief that a school leader can approve.

  • School type, curriculum, classes and campuses in scope.
  • Current documents, approval route and reporting calendar.
  • Number of teachers, administrators and learners affected.
  • Available devices, internet, power and technical support.
  • Privacy, safeguarding and record-retention rules.
  • Training dates and staff availability.
  • Success measures for the pilot period.
  • Budget owner and final decision maker.

Software should not be approved only because a demonstration looks impressive. Ask who owns the data, how users receive support, whether outputs can be exported, how errors are corrected, and what happens if the school stops using the service.

Frequently asked questions

Abuja and the FCT school software questions

Does JENECONK have an office in Abuja and the FCT?

No permanent Abuja and the FCT office is claimed. Remote support is available, and onsite work is considered by written agreement.

Can Edu Suite replace teacher review?

No. Edu Suite creates drafts and working materials. Teachers and school leaders remain responsible for accuracy, curriculum fit, learner context and final approval.

Can the school start with a small pilot?

Yes. A limited pilot with named users, a defined workflow and measurable review criteria is normally more useful than immediate school-wide deployment.

Is CBT a public subscription product?

No. JENECONK handles CBT as a scoped institutional setup service after candidate volume, venue, delivery mode, reporting and support requirements are understood.

School owners and administrators should confirm current regulatory, examination and education requirements directly with the responsible authorities. JENECONK is an independent technology company and does not replace government guidance.
