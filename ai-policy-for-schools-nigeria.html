AI Governance

How Nigerian Schools Can Create a Simple AI Policy

A practical AI policy guide for Nigerian schools covering privacy, teacher use, learner safety, academic honesty, and approved tools.

Open Education SolutionsBrowse resources

Updated 2026-08-04 by JENECONK

Why this matters

Schools need AI adoption rules before confusion becomes a problem. The practical aim is simple: better documents, cleaner records, safer systems, stronger learning, and faster decisions.

This guide keeps the advice practical. It is designed for people who need to do real work this week, whether they are teachers preparing classes, administrators managing records, business owners serving customers, or learners building new skills.

Practical steps

  1. Define what teachers may use AI for, including planning, reports, visuals, admin drafts, and assessment support.
  2. Separate allowed learner use from restricted exam or assignment use.
  3. Protect learner data by banning unnecessary personal details in public AI tools.
  4. Require human review before any AI-generated material is published or shared.
  5. Train staff so the policy is understood in practice, not just saved as a document.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Banning AI without teaching staff how to handle it.
  • Allowing every tool without privacy review.
  • Treating AI output as automatically correct.
