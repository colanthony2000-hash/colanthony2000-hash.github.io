Professional document workspace

Business Suite

Guided workflows for CVs, proposals, official letters, certificates, reports and other polished professional documents. Review every detail before sharing or submitting.

Best for: Individuals and organisations preparing clear, professional documents.

Household and small-business money tracker

Record income, sales and expenses; follow balances; plan savings and bills; track debts; and keep daily money tasks in one workspace. Entries are made by the user; the service does not automatically connect to a bank.
