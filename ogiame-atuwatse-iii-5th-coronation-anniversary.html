Iwere Nation | 14-23 August 2026

Celebrating five remarkable years of the reign of Ogiame Atuwatse III.

JENECONK joins Itsekiri people at home and around the world in honouring His Majesty Ogiame Atuwatse III, CFR, the Olu of Warri, on the occasion of his 5th Coronation Anniversary.

Ogiame Suo!Iwere Suo!

A message from JENECONK

With pride, gratitude and a deep sense of belonging.

As a company with roots in the Itsekiri community, JENECONK proudly celebrates this important milestone with Iwere Nation. The anniversary is an opportunity to honour our heritage, strengthen the bonds that connect our people, and look ahead with confidence.

His Majesty's reign continues to represent a call to unity, peace, purposeful leadership and progress. These values matter across generations and speak to the future we all share.

May Almighty God continue to grant our King wisdom, strength and long life on the revered throne of his forebears, and may his reign continue to usher in greater peace, unity and progress for Iwere Nation.

Five years on the throne.

His Majesty Ogiame Atuwatse III was crowned on 21 August 2021 as the 21st Olu of Warri. JENECONK extends warm congratulations to the Royal Family, the Warri Council of Chiefs, and Itsekiri sons and daughters everywhere during the 2026 anniversary celebrations.

Learn more from the official website of the Olu of Warri.
