Frequently asked

Quick answers.

Can AI write a complete lesson note?

AI can prepare a strong draft, but a teacher should review it for curriculum fit, class level, local examples, and school requirements.

Does Edu Suite replace teachers?

No. Edu Suite supports teacher preparation so the teacher can spend more energy on delivery, examples, and learner support.

Better inputs

Prepare the lesson before asking for a draft.

Enter the class, subject, topic, curriculum or scheme reference, lesson duration, learner level, available materials and required lesson-note format. Add the learning objective if the school already specifies one. Vague input produces generic output, while accurate context makes review faster.

After generation, verify objectives, facts, examples, timing, activities, differentiation, evaluation and assignment. Replace unavailable resources and unfamiliar contexts. Check that the evaluation tests what the lesson actually taught. A complete-looking draft can still be unteachable.

Example request

“Create a 40-minute 5E lesson note for Basic 6 Mathematics on equivalent fractions. Learners can identify numerator and denominator but struggle to compare fractions. Available materials are fraction strips and a whiteboard. Include measurable objectives, group activity, formative checks and a five-question evaluation.”

Do not include learner names, personal records or confidential assessment material. The final lesson note remains the responsibility of the teacher and school.
