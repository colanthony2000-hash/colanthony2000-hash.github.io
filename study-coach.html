A clearer revision routine

Answer, understand and try again.

Practice is most useful when a learner can see why an answer is right or wrong. Study Coach combines questions with immediate explanations so each session can become a learning cycle, not only a score.

Learners can use a quick topic drill when time is limited, then move to a broader examination session when they are ready to test accuracy, stamina and recall across more questions.

A practical study-session example

Use the result to decide what happens next.

A learner preparing for a Biology paper might begin with a short topic drill to check recall, then attempt a diagram-identification set. After reviewing the explanations, the learner can write down two weak concepts, return to a textbook or class note, and repeat a fresh set later. A JAMB candidate can follow the same pattern across the four subjects connected to an intended course.

1. Set one purpose

Choose a topic, examination or course combination instead of opening a random session without a revision goal.

2. Work without guessing

Answer carefully, flag uncertainty in personal notes and avoid rushing simply to reach the final score.

3. Review the reasoning

Read every explanation for a missed question and identify whether the problem was knowledge, interpretation or timing.

4. Close the gap

Study the weak concept from a trusted source, ask for teacher support where needed and schedule a later practice session.

Use it responsibly

A study aid, not an examination promise.

AI Study Coach is designed for learning and revision. Learners should still use their current syllabus, trusted textbooks, school guidance and official examination-body information when planning preparation.

AI-generated practice content can contain mistakes or may not match the exact emphasis of a future paper. Review explanations critically, ask a teacher when something is unclear and treat results as practice indicators rather than official forecasts.

Independent platformStudy Coach is not owned, endorsed or operated by WAEC, JAMB or NECO.
No guaranteed scorePractice can support preparation, but it cannot guarantee marks, admission or any examination outcome.
Teacher guidance still mattersQualified teachers remain important for correcting misconceptions and building a balanced study plan.

Questions and answers

What learners and parents should know.

Which examinations does AI Study Coach support?

The current live platform provides direct practice pathways for WAEC, JAMB UTME and NECO, together with standard topic drills.

How many questions can a learner practise?

Flexible practice lengths currently range from 5 to 50 questions. The JAMB course-combination pathway also presents longer-format options inside the live application.

What are JAMB course combinations?

A learner selects an intended course, and the platform presents a standard four-subject combination for practice. Learners should still confirm current entry requirements with JAMB and their chosen institution.

Is the platform official or affiliated with an examination body?

No. AI Study Coach is an independent JENECONK Digital Academy product. It is not an official WAEC, JAMB or NECO platform and does not reproduce a future examination paper.

Can parents or schools use it alongside teaching?

Yes. It can support revision at home, guided practice in a school or a supervised bootcamp. A teacher can use practice results as one signal when deciding what to revise next.
