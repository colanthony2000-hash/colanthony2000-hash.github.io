From lesson-note headings to a teachable plan

A usable lesson note gives the teacher a clear path through the period. It connects what learners should learn with what the teacher will explain, what learners will practise, which resources are available and how understanding will be checked.

The guide takes readers through the components of a classroom-ready lesson note, interpreting curriculum requirements, writing measurable objectives, organising content, planning introductions and presentations, and aligning evaluation and assignments with the lesson. It also covers adapting plans to different learners and common lesson-note writing errors.

What makes this more than a short checklist?

The digital book is positioned as a practical guide and workbook. It includes worked examples, a planning laboratory, exercises, reusable templates, classroom guidance and an answer guide, so readers can practise the method as they learn it. The accompanying activities can help student teachers prepare for teaching practice and help practising teachers review their approach.

Who is the guide for?

  • Primary and secondary school teachers preparing classroom lessons.
  • Student teachers and trainee teachers learning to plan instruction.
  • Teachers preparing for teaching practice.
  • Newly qualified educators building a reliable preparation routine.
  • Education students and others developing lesson-planning skills.
