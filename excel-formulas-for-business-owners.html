Excel Formulas Nigerian Business Owners Should Know

Useful Excel formulas for small business owners managing sales, expenses, invoices, stock, customers, and monthly reports.

Open Business HubBrowse resources

Updated 2026-08-04 by JENECONK

Why this matters

Business owners do not need every formula; they need the right formulas for decisions. The practical aim is simple: better documents, cleaner records, safer systems, stronger learning, and faster decisions.

This guide keeps the advice practical. It is designed for people who need to do real work this week, whether they are teachers preparing classes, administrators managing records, business owners serving customers, or learners building new skills.

Practical steps

  1. Use SUMIFS to summarize sales or expenses by category and period.
  2. Use XLOOKUP to pull prices, customer details, and product information.
  3. Use IF to flag low stock, overdue invoices, or missing information.
  4. Use TEXT and date formulas to format periods and deadlines.
  5. Use pivot tables to compare products, locations, and months.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Mixing personal and business expenses in one unclear sheet.
  • Deleting old records instead of filtering by date.
  • Relying on color alone to mean status.
