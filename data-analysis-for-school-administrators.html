Data Analysis for School Administrators

How school administrators can use data analysis for attendance, fees, results, staffing, parent communication, and planning.

Open Education HubBrowse resources

Updated 2026-08-04 by JENECONK

Why this matters

School data should help leaders see problems early and act with confidence. The practical aim is simple: better documents, cleaner records, safer systems, stronger learning, and faster decisions.

This guide keeps the advice practical. It is designed for people who need to do real work this week, whether they are teachers preparing classes, administrators managing records, business owners serving customers, or learners building new skills.

Practical steps

  1. Track attendance patterns by class, week, and term.
  2. Monitor fees by payment status and follow-up priority.
  3. Analyze results by subject, teacher, class, and assessment type.
  4. Use report summaries to identify learners needing support.
  5. Review data during management meetings, not only at term end.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Keeping records only for compliance.
  • Treating low scores as learner failure without checking teaching and assessment patterns.
  • Waiting too long before acting on attendance or fee signals.
