Procurement workflow and accountability

Your procurement request is delayed. Know exactly who is holding it.

Smart Procurement turns fragmented emails, spreadsheets and manual approvals into one traceable workflow. From request to payment, authorised teams can see the current owner, stage age, outstanding action and complete history.

The management blind spot

Disconnected tools make delay ownership difficult to see.

When requests move through email threads, Excel trackers, chats, calls and manual signatures, management often has to chase updates. Important evidence becomes scattered, the current owner is unclear and cycle-time reporting depends on manual reconstruction.

Immediate statusSee where a request is, who currently owns it and when it entered that stage.
SLA visibilityTrack stage age and identify approvals that have exceeded the organisation's expected processing time.
Historical auditReview previous owners, decisions, timestamps and supporting documents across the lifecycle.

Controlled pipeline

One workflow from request to final closure.

The sequence can be configured around an organisation's operating and approval structure while preserving the evidence needed for oversight.

  2. Structured capture

Intake

Controlled email capture

Sourcing

RFQ and vendor processing

Completion

Full-cycle tracking

Tracking continues after the PO stage through delivery, invoice processing, payment follow-up and closure.

Works around existing systems

Keep your ERP. Control the operational workflow around it.

Smart Procurement is designed to manage the operational steps that surround an ERP or accounting system. Controlled handoffs or API integrations can be considered where available; the current implementation includes a managed Sage/PO checkpoint.

Deployment scope, integrations, security controls and approval paths are agreed for each organisation. The public UAT link is for authorised users and demonstrations, not open self-registration.
