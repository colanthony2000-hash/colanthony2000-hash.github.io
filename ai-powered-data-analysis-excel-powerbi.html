Data Analysis and AI

AI-Powered Data Analysis with Excel and Power BI

How AI can support data cleaning, formula help, dashboard planning, insight writing, and reporting in Excel and Power BI.

Why this matters

AI can help analysts work faster, but the data and questions still need human judgment. The practical aim is simple: better documents, cleaner records, safer systems, stronger learning, and faster decisions.

This guide keeps the advice practical. It is designed for people who need to do real work this week, whether they are teachers preparing classes, administrators managing records, business owners serving customers, or learners building new skills.

Practical steps

  1. Use AI to explain formulas, DAX measures, and cleaning steps.
  2. Ask AI to suggest dashboard questions before building visuals.
  3. Generate first drafts of insight summaries from verified numbers.
  4. Use AI to check whether charts match the question being asked.
  5. Protect confidential data when using AI-assisted analysis.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Pasting sensitive datasets into unapproved tools.
  • Accepting AI interpretations without checking the source data.
  • Asking AI for insights before cleaning and validating records.
