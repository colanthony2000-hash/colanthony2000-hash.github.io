A scoped implementation service

The right CBT setup depends on candidates, connectivity, and control.

A school entrance examination with fifty candidates does not need the same architecture as a recruitment assessment across several locations. JENECONK begins with the examination purpose, candidate volume, question workflow, available devices, internet reliability, result requirements, and the staff who will administer the session.

Online delivery can support remote or distributed candidates where connectivity and identity controls are adequate. A local network or offline approach can be more dependable for a controlled venue with limited internet. The recommended route is agreed after the environment is understood; no delivery mode is presented as universally suitable.

Question ownership remains with the institution. The setup process should define who prepares questions, who reviews them, when access opens, how incidents are handled, and who may see or export results.
