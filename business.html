Choose the right starting point

Business Suite creates documents. Smart Procurement controls a process.

Use Business Suite when the immediate need is a professional output such as a CV, proposal, invoice, official letter, company profile, agreement, report, certificate, or complete document pack. The guided workspace collects the facts, assembles a structured draft, and lets the user review the result before unlocking a clean export.

Use Smart Procurement when several people must move a request through defined stages. It records the current owner, approval age, RFQ activity, delivery follow-up, invoice status, payment follow-up, and the history of the procurement cycle. It is an implementation-led organisational system rather than a public self-service generator.

Training and consulting support both pathways. JENECONK can help a team define its document standards, prepare staff to use AI responsibly, or map an operational workflow before software configuration begins.

Before you begin

Prepare the facts that make business work credible.

Technology can improve structure and speed, but responsibility for names, figures, claims, approvals, and final use remains with the person or organisation producing the work.

Document factsGather correct names, dates, prices, responsibilities, evidence, and contact details before drafting.
Approval routeIdentify who requests, reviews, approves, purchases, receives, and confirms payment.
Human reviewCheck generated language, calculations, legal implications, and organisation-specific requirements.
Controlled recordsKeep final versions, approval evidence, and source documents in the authorised business system.
