JENECONK training

How JENECONK turns learning into guided practice.

This short clip introduces the practical side of JENECONK training. Every program should give learners a clear task, a chance to practise and a next step they can apply in school, work or a real project.

What to expect

Clear instruction, useful practice and a pathway onward.

Training topics include AI productivity, office tools, Excel and data analysis, SQL, web design, cybersecurity awareness, digital skills and project management. The right course depends on the learner's present task and the outcome they want to build.

Explore training programsUse free learning guides

Choose a useful next step

Start with one skill and a real task.

For data work, begin with the Power BI DAX foundations lesson or the SQL foundations guide. For AI-supported work, begin with the AI and prompt-engineering guide. These resources are available before enrolment so prospective learners can make a more informed choice.
